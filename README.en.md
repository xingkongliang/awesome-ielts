> A curated list of **official, public, and open-source** IELTS resources. **No pirated Cambridge papers.**

中文版：[README.md](README.md)

IELTS is jointly owned by the [British Council](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org), [IDP IELTS](https://ielts.idp.com), and [Cambridge University Press & Assessment](https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/ielts/). Many popular GitHub lists still point at leaked Cambridge book PDFs. Those links are copyright infringement, and the books they dump are often a decade old. This list only includes resources you can open legally.

Maintained by [EasyIELTS](https://easyielts.cn/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=awesome_list&utm_campaign=open_ielts_2026_08), a Chinese-language speaking and writing practice product. **Not affiliated with or endorsed by the IELTS partners.** Product links live under practice/feedback, not under Official.

## Start here (30 minutes)

1. Read the official [Speaking band descriptors (PDF)](https://ielts.org/cdn/ielts-guides/ielts-speaking-band-descriptors.pdf) and [Writing band descriptors (PDF)](https://ielts.org/cdn/Guides/ielts-writing-band-descriptors.pdf).
2. Sit the [Academic sample questions](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/preparation-resources/sample-test-questions/academic-test).
3. If you will take IELTS on computer, run the [British Council familiarisation test](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/prepare/ielts-free-practice-mock-tests/ielts-familiarisation-test).
4. Speak: record one Part 1 answer. Listening and Reading have answer keys; Speaking and Writing do not — you need feedback.

Approximate raw scores ([source](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/your-results/ielts-scoring-in-detail); each version varies slightly):

| Band | Listening /40 | Academic Reading /40 | GT Reading /40 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | 16 | 15 | 23 |
| 6 | 23 | 23 | 30 |
| 7 | 30 | 30 | 35 |
| 8 | 35 | 35 | — |

Overall band = average of four skills, rounded to the nearest half band (`.25` up to `.5`, `.75` up to the next whole).

## Skills

**Listening:** official samples · [BC mocks](https://takeielts.britishcouncil.org/prepare/ielts-free-practice-mock-tests/academic) · [LearnEnglish Listening](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/skills/listening) · [BBC Learning English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish) · [6 Minute English](https://www.bbc.co.uk/learningenglish/english/features/6-minute-english) · [TED](https://www.ted.com/talks) · [ELLLO](https://www.elllo.org) · [Speech Accent Archive](https://accent.gmu.edu)

**Writing:** descriptors + examiner-commented samples · [Academic Phrasebank](https://www.phrasebank.manchester.ac.uk) · [Purdue OWL](https://owl.purdue.edu) · [Write & Improve](https://writeandimprove.com) · [IELTS Liz Writing](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-writing-task-2/) · [IELTS Simon](https://ielts-simon.com) · [IELTS Advantage](https://www.ieltsadvantage.com/) · [EasyIELTS writing practice](https://easyielts.cn/writing-practice?utm_source=github&utm_medium=awesome_list&utm_campaign=open_ielts_2026_08) (Chinese UI, four-criteria scoring)

**Speaking:** descriptors · official sample videos · [Speak & Improve](https://speakandimprove.com) · [IELTS Liz Speaking](https://ieltsliz.com/ielts-speaking-free-lessons-essential-tips/) · [YouGlish](https://youglish.com) · [EasyIELTS speaking](https://easyielts.cn/speaking-practice?utm_source=github&utm_medium=awesome_list&utm_campaign=open_ielts_2026_08) (AI interviewer, four-criteria feedback)

Seasonal “topic pools” circulating on social media are **not** published by IELTS. Useful as prompts; memorised answers get penalised.

## Mainland China booking

In mainland China the test is run by NEEA + British Council. **IDP is not the booking site.**

- [ielts.neea.cn](https://ielts.neea.cn/) — the only registration, payment, speaking-slot, and admit-card system
- [chinaielts.org](https://www.chinaielts.org/) — BC China information, [fees](https://www.chinaielts.org/about-ielts/ielts), [paper-test end dates](https://www.chinaielts.org/about-ielts/ways-to-take-ielts/paper/iop-test-date)

Elsewhere: book via British Council or IDP.

## What to buy (legal)

When free samples are not enough: recent **Cambridge IELTS** volumes (16+), *The Official Cambridge Guide to IELTS*, and *Official IELTS Practice Materials*. Do not download the books from GitHub.

## What this list refuses

Pirated Cambridge audio/PDFs, full scans of commercial Chinese prep books, “guaranteed band” landing pages, and anything behind a reseller’s WeChat QR.
